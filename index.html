Android Phone Setup

Chrome PWA

How to open and update your tracker from anywhere on your Android phone:

1. Open this URL in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
2. Tap Chrome's Three Dots Menu (⋮) at top right.
3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

System Upgrades Roadmap

Options to scale your tracker into a full cloud-synchronized ecosystem:

1. Google Sheets Auto-Sync Free Cloud DB

Sync daily check-ins to a Google Sheet via Apps Script. Canva can link to Google Sheets via Bulk Create to update your design graphics automatically.

2. Native Android APK (Capacitor) Stand-alone App

Package with Capacitor into a standalone Android `.apk` for Google Play, with native home-screen widgets, lock screen glanceables, and biometric security.

3. Cloud Auth & Multi-Device Sync Supabase

Connect Supabase PostgreSQL backend for instant live sync between your Android phone, tablet, and laptop with zero data loss.
